About CareStar

Member-owned. Specialist-managed.

CareStar was licensed in 2026 as a risk retention group — an insurance company owned by the insureds it serves. Coverage is written on general and professional liability forms tailored to human services exposures, supported by independent actuarial analysis, dedicated claims handling, and specialized loss control.

The program is managed by CareStar Manager LLC.
